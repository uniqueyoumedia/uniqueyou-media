Why this matters now

Four reasons not to wait.

AI makes everything sound the same

Anyone can generate text and images in seconds. Your story and the way you work cannot be copied. That is your difference now.

Buyers are roughly 61 percent through their decision before they speak to anyone.1 If they find nothing, they quietly pick someone else.

What success looks like

Being the one they think of first.

The nicest feedback we get never comes from our clients. It comes from their customers, who tell them: you are the same online as you are here. Same energy, same way of talking. Honestly, that is the whole job.

What comes after that is slower than a viral moment and worth a lot more. People drop in more often. They buy more often. At some point you are simply not a bistro or a winery any more. You are the one people name when they think of your trade.

That takes months, not weeks. Which is why we would rather work with you for a year than for six weeks.

What we stand for

Marketing that feels good, not loud.

If social media currently makes you tense, that is the first thing we want to change. Growth here happens step by step, in an order that cannot be skipped.

First

Being visible, honestly and regularly

Not a campaign, a rhythm. People need to see you more than once, and they need to see the real you, otherwise none of the rest works.

Then

Trust, with the people watching

Followers and customers start recognising how you think and how you work. That part takes months and no shortcut exists for it.

And then

Sales, as a consequence

They come on their own once the first two are in place. We will never promise you numbers by next month. Anyone who does is selling you something else.

Pulling at the grass does not make it grow faster.

This only works as well as you join in. It is a project we do together, not something you hand over and collect later.

If it is meant to be authentic, it has to come from your own head and your own heart. You built the vision for your business, so that part cannot be outsourced, not to us and not to anyone.

We take the tech and everything around it: editing, design, planning, publishing. Exactly the hours that otherwise eat your evening. As your sparring partners we then keep you going, even when the business is demanding. We are the tailwind, not the engine.

More answers
„I have no time for this.“

That is the point of working with us. You film short moments from the day you are having anyway, a minute here and there. We do the editing, the writing, the planning and the publishing. The part that eats your evenings is the part we take.

„I feel ridiculous in front of a camera.“

Almost everyone does at the start, including people who speak on stage without blinking. We do not ask you to perform. We ask you questions and film the answer, and we keep going until it sounds like you talking, not like an advert.
