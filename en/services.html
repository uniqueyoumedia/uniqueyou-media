Let’s talk

Services and prices

Three ways to work with us. Prices included.

A lot of agencies only tell you a number after a sales call. We would rather you knew it right now, so our first conversation can be about your business instead of your budget.

Our one rule

You should never need us.

We hand over everything we know. Then you choose whether you still want us.

Our whole philosophy is that you are not dependent on us. You get the knowledge, the method and the craft, and you could run it all yourself from day one.

Most people keep us on anyway, and the reason is a simple one. It saves them time and nerves, and having someone check in every week is what stops the whole thing sinking into the everyday chaos of running a business. They stay because they want to, never because they have to.

01

The workshop

We teach you the whole craft, so you could do this entirely without us.

We go through what you have tried and where you got stuck. Then we work on the parts that actually matter: what you want to be known for, what to post, how to handle your phone as a camera, and what to say once it is recording.

You practise live while we are there, so it does not stay theory. Most people record and publish their first real video on the same day.

  • Right for you ifYou want to understand it yourself and stop depending on anyone for it.
  • WhereOnline with both of us, or on site at your place when that makes more sense.
  • You leave withYour positioning, your first videos, and a content plan you can keep using.
  • PriceFrom 1,297 euro net.
02

The image film

One film that shows who you are, filmed with you, in your own space.

Three preparation calls before we film, each with a script we rework for you. We look at your rooms, your light and the way you move in them, and then we film the version of you that your regulars already know.

This is the piece that sits on your website and does the talking when you are not there. It is also where most of our clients started with us.

  • Right for you ifPeople get you once they meet you, and the website is not doing that job.
  • WhereOn location at your business. We travel, including outside Germany.
  • You getThe main film plus short cut-downs for social media.
  • PriceFrom 2,897 euro net, depending on filming days and travel.
03

Ongoing support

You film the raw moments. We do everything that happens after.

You film on your phone and send us the material, or we record it together in a short call. We do the editing, the captions, the planning and the publishing, written in your own voice so it still sounds like you.

We also stay your sparring partners week by week, because on your own this is the thing that quietly slips. You see everything before it goes out, so you stay in control of what the world sees.

  • Right for you ifYour time is tight and you want to be visible without doing the admin.
  • WhereFully remote and ongoing, in any time zone.
  • RhythmA weekly check-in, plus us reachable in between.
  • PriceFrom 547 euro net a month.

How long this takes

Six weeks will not do it.

Visibility is not a campaign. People need months before they recognise you, and the routines that make posting feel light need about as long to settle.

That is why ongoing support has a minimum term of three months, and why we will tell you honestly that six to nine is where it actually starts working. Anything shorter only scratches the surface, and we would rather say that before you pay us than after.

The other half of it is you. This works as well as you join in, because authentic content has to come from your own head. We take the tech, the time and the pressure, and we keep you going. The vision stays yours.
