What we hear most

„I know I should be doing this. I just don't know where to start.“

The people we work with are genuinely good at what they do. Years of experience, real results, clients who love them. Online they can look like someone who started last week, and that is not their fault. Nobody ever showed them how.

So we do not start with the algorithm. We start with you, with what you want to be known for, and with the smallest step that gets you moving. We will never promise you a number of followers. What we do promise is that you will understand what you are doing and why.

We don't build accounts. We build people who become visible.

How we can work together

Two ways in.

01

The live workshop, online

A day together on screen, and you finally understand how this works for you.

We go through what you have tried so far and where you got stuck. Then we work on the parts that actually matter: what you want to be known for, what to post, how to handle your phone as a camera, and what to say once it is recording.

You practise live while we are on the call, so it does not stay theory. Most people record and publish their first real video on the same day. You leave with a plan you can keep using without us.

  • Right for you ifYou want to understand it yourself and stop depending on anyone for it.
  • FormatLive online with both of us, split into blocks so it stays manageable.
  • You leave withYour positioning, your first videos, and a content plan.
02

Remote support

You keep running your business. We take the rest off your plate.

You film on your phone and send us the material, or we record it together in a short call. We do the editing, the captions, the planning and the posting, written in your own voice so it still sounds like you.

You see everything before it goes out, so you stay in control of what the world sees. There is nothing technical to set up on your side, and you can reach us whenever something comes up.

  • Right for you ifYour time is tight and you want to be visible without doing it yourself.
  • FormatFully remote and ongoing, in any time zone.
  • You getPosts, reels and captions, planned ahead and approved by you.
