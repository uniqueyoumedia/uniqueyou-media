How it started

With one honest question.

„You absolutely light up when you talk about this. You don’t do that with your other job.“

We met while Alex was still studying in South Korea, both of us travelling. We talked a lot about how much we love being on the road. Then he went home, back into his everyday life as a chemical engineer.

One day he told me he really loves doing videography, and loves travelling, but first he would have to work at the company for another two years before he could take enough time off for a longer trip.

So I asked him: why not make videography your job? You absolutely light up when you talk about it. You do not do that with the other one, even though you are good at it.

That is where all of this started.

Then it snowballed

Two reactions to one film.

I come from the health world originally. I am a mindset and health mentor and a nutrition coach for business owners. In June 2024 I asked Alex to make an image film for me and my business, and I got two kinds of reaction to it.

One: I would love to work with you. Two: who made that film?

The answer was Alex and me, because I have loved photography for many, many years and I have always had a pull towards marketing, creativity and design.

From there, requests for image films started coming in through my network. The clients were simply happy. They felt understood, seen and heard, and they could finally get their vision out into the world. All of them were already successful offline. They just did not have the time, the headspace, or sometimes the confidence to show that online.

Then the people we had made films for asked whether we could help them with social media too. That is how everything took its course.

Where we are now

Work and travel, in the same week.

These days we get to do this worldwide, which means we can combine travelling with our work. We have had projects in Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Portugal, Tenerife and Asia.

We absolutely love what we do.

What drives us is this: there is a lot of rubbish and a lot of fake out there on social media. We think there should be far more accounts showing real businesses, bringing genuine value, being properly unique. Those are the ones that deserve the reach and the customers.

Mostly it just takes a bit of tailwind, and that is the part we can give you.
