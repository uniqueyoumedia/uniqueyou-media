Questions we get every week

The honest answers.

„I have no time for this.“

That is exactly what we take off you. You film short moments from the day you are having anyway, a minute here and there, in the kitchen, on the way to a site, while you set something up.

Everything after that is ours: the editing, the writing, the planning, the publishing. The part that eats your evenings is the part you hand over.

„I feel ridiculous in front of a camera.“

Almost everyone does at the start, including people who speak on a stage without blinking. That is completely normal. It is simply unfamiliar.

We do not ask you to perform. We ask you questions and film the answers, and we keep going until it sounds like you talking rather than an advert. Being steadily less uncomfortable is the actual skill here, and it is learnable.

„It is probably too expensive for me.“

Our prices are published, which is more than most agencies will tell you before a sales call. A workshop starts at 1,297 euro, an image film at 2,897 euro and ongoing support at 547 euro a month, all net.

If that is not your range, you will know after half an hour instead of after three meetings. We would honestly rather tell you that early than take up your whole afternoon.

„Do you only work remotely?“

Almost everything runs remotely, which is why where you are makes no difference to us. We have worked with clients in Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Portugal, Tenerife and Asia.

When it genuinely helps, we come to you, for a workshop on site or to film. Everything after that continues digitally.

„How long until I see something?“

Content goes out in the first weeks. The thing you actually want, people recognising you and coming to you, takes months.

We would rather say that plainly than sell you a number. Minimum term on ongoing support is three months, and six to nine is where it starts to compound.

„Who exactly will I be working with?“

Marina and Alex. Both of us, every time. There is nobody else in the company, so there are no account managers in between and nothing gets handed to a junior.

„What happens to my data?“

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties, so simply reading this page sends your data nowhere. There is no sign-up form either. Booking a call goes through Calendly, with a data processing agreement in place.

Material from your business stays yours. Nothing is published without you seeing it first. The details are in our privacy policy.
